# Fresh Eyes Find Bugs

Context is blindness. The author of an artifact is the worst reviewer of
it.

- Reviewers get the artifact and the standard, nothing else.
- Never send parent history to a reviewer.
- No spawn tool means a labeled self-review. Never claim fresh eyes you
  did not use.

Sources: qa-delegate skill; QA Stack design spec.
