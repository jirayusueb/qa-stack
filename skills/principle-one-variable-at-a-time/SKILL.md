# One Variable at a Time

An experiment that changes two things answers nothing. Attribution needs
isolation.

- One change, one rerun, one recorded observation.
- Killed and confirmed hypotheses both get their deciding observation.
- Bisect is this principle applied to versions.

Sources: qa-diagnose skill; QA Stack design spec.
