# Team variant

Teammates are durable. A brief becomes a task on the shared board.

1. Create the task with the brief fields as the description.
2. Record the write scope on the task. The board warns on overlap.
3. Assign the stage to one member. One writer per file.
4. The member completes the task with the artifact path in the result.
5. The Lead reviews the diff and runs the checks. A task is done when its
   checks pass, not when its member stops.

## Fresh-context review on a team

Pick a member who has not touched the artifact. If every member has touched
it, the Lead reviews and says so. Team review by the author is forbidden.

## Blocking

A blocked task goes back to pending with the blocking condition in the
description. Name the missing artifact or the busy writer. Never block
silently and never take over another member's write scope.
